About Us

Our Story

At tesetmart, we pride ourselves on being a cornerstone of the community. Whether you need a quick satisfying dinner, a cold drink on a hot day, or to pick up some last-minute essentials, we are here for you. We started with a simple mission: to provide high-quality, freshly cooked food with a smile.


Our fish is locally sourced, our chips are cut thick, and our burgers are made with 100% Australian beef. We believe in good food, good portions, and great value.


  • Fresh, quality ingredients every day
  • Fast and friendly service
  • Convenience store items available
  • Easy parking access
